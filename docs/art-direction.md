# Elsewhere ? art and level-design direction

User direction adopted 30 September 2026. This guides blockout and production decisions; [chapter-one.md](chapter-one.md) distinguishes current implementation from the intended experience.

## Recognizable outside, inhabitable inside

The 1 October reference clarifies the next experiment: consolidate around one architectural object whose beams and passages are the puzzle. A surrounding enclosure is not inherently needed. [The turning frame](monument-study.md) tests one main form, central opening, moving assembly and destination while preserving the courtyard for comparison. Integrate curved transitions into the construction. Do not copy the reference's dome, flag or palette as a substitute for this relationship. Keep walking widths usable and inspect both distant composition and walking-height presence. The new underside is still sparse in first person; this is an open design issue.

Create a coherent world identifiable in isometric view and experienced through architecture, proportions, light, materials, sound and recurring details in first person. The Rose's Room reference concerns the relationship between distant recognition and immersion, not a literal toy-room setting. A toy planetarium is an option, not an approved theme.

Large forms and their arrangement must communicate the identity before decoration. Articulated rings, galleries, structural ribs, luminous chambers and shared bases should determine how routes fit together. Adding symbols to unrelated platforms does not satisfy this direction.

Use deliberate negative space: a courtyard, framed void, tower opening or gap between architectural shells. Routes should inhabit these structures as terraces, inner passages, exterior ledges and galleries. A curve should lead beneath a recognizable structure, not merely invert the character for its own sake.

Keep recurring references across changes of ground. Let traversal reveal the same structure differently, and let returning to overview explain how an inhabited passage belongs to the larger form. Reaching a previously visible destination is a spatial reward.

## Readability

Use strong silhouettes, controlled detail and selective lighting. Movable pieces have distinct, persistent assembly colours, reinforced by shape and labels. Do not encode XYZ or change identity when gravity changes. Fixed architecture, walking surfaces and mechanical joints must remain distinguishable in both views.

The current sample uses terracotta, violet and teal for the three moving assemblies; blue-grey for fixed structure; ivory paving; brass joints. This is a sample palette to test, not a claim of validated accessibility.

Walkable boundaries should follow physical architecture. Avoid large apparently walkable surfaces restricted to an unexplained stripe. Recommended isometric composition must show the player, active assembly and references. Contextual interior reveals remain an available technique, not a remedy for needlessly confusing composition. Keep complete models except when such a reveal is warranted.

## Relationships before sequences

The central question remains: **Where do I need to be before I transform the space?** Solutions depend jointly on configuration, occupied surface and order. Impossible geometry remains optional.

Repeatedly boarding, rotating and continuing must not be the universal strategy. A useful connection may remove another; the access route may consume turning clearance; a structure may need to move after the player leaves it. A short solution can require substantial reasoning without extra branches, longer walks or mandatory levers.

Remote manipulation is part of the rules. Preparation only matters when a real condition makes it necessary: path access, different grounds, lost connections, a justified local interaction, or genuine physical interference. Never impose an arbitrary camera/manipulation limit to manufacture order.

Define the expected first approach, why it is insufficient and the intended discovery before building. The current test is: **the gallery used to enter must be withdrawn before the ring can turn**. Visible docking cheeks and sampled sweeps implement this relationship; they are not a requirement to add collision locks everywhere.

Exploration should expose relationships, disconnected paths or another view of a destination. Revisits should change understanding or opportunities, not require walking back merely to activate a switch. A circular route is useful only if the return matters.

## Experimentation and immediate priority

Support prediction, testing, specific feedback and revision. Failure is not required: an observant first-attempt solution may demonstrate reasoning. Rejected turns identify the obstruction; undo, saved progress and physical recovery keep experimentation practical.

Chapter structure: a brief movement/curved-ground/manipulation introduction, one compact problem with interdependent pieces, a changed application, then a spatial reward. Existing systems and selectable prototypes are retained; the previous chapter layout is not a production constraint.

Develop architectural form and puzzle logic together. Before full visual production, test whether the overview suggests a coherent place, first person feels inhabitable, position and order have real consequences, and visiting mechanisms sequentially is insufficient. Human evidence is still pending. Sound, finished materials and environmental detail follow this validation rather than substituting for it.
